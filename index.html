Meet Me

A designer focused on untangling complexity to present the truth.

I'm operating somewhere between a designer, artist and chronic experimenter. Currently doing that as a founder and Creative Director at Calumma. This website is my "Library of Everything" — a living archive of how my brain works and what I enjoy pursuing.

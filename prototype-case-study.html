Print & Craft

Hard Rock Hotels

A hand-crafted paper record player
for very important guests

Year 2019
Client Hard Rock Hotels
Industry Hospitality
Tags Print, Craft, Experiential

Hard Rock Hotels asked for a custom welcome gift for selected high-profile guests. Several ideas of varying complexity were presented — the client loved the idea of a bespoke paper vinyl player. From that brief came prototypes, cut fingers, broken vinyl records, and one of the most satisfying things ever made in a studio.

Challenge

Create something genuinely surprising for guests who have already seen everything. The gift needed to feel premium, embody the Hard Rock music heritage, and work within an extremely short timeframe.

Approach

A paper record player sleeve that guests can fold and play. Prototyping, material sourcing and testing happened simultaneously. A personalised welcome message and a custom track were recorded and pressed to vinyl.

Outcome

A functional paper record player — fold the sleeve, place the vinyl, spin it at 45rpm. Completely analog, completely unexpected. A welcome gift that got people talking.

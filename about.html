Meet Me

I specialise in curiosity.

My creative pursuits started with copying comic book characters in my primary school homework notebooks and dreaming of working for Cartoon Network on series like Cow and Chicken, Dexter's Laboratory, and I Am Weasel. In early teens, I was designing pixel art on my Nokia 3310 and digitally composing my friends into surreal collages using a pirated copy of Paint Shop Pro 8.

In my late teens, I made stencil art, spent time in a darkroom developing film photography, acted in a local theatre group, and designed nightclub posters. In my early 20s, I ran a tattoo parlour in my living room, gave my mum her first tattoo, and had my design work in 400+ tech stores across the UK before I landed my first full-time job in the creative industry.

For the past 15 years, I've been blessed to work across brand identity, commercial art direction, digital experiences, packaging design, video production, print publications, illustration and interactive media.

In 2019, I co-founded Daydream Believers, helping build it into an award-winning educational organisation recognised as one of the top 100 education innovators in the world by HundrED, working in partnership with LEGO, Rockstar North, D&AD, and the Ellen MacArthur Foundation.

In 2021, I founded Calumma, a creative studio for rebellious-optimist-led organisations tackling challenges across education, healthcare, tech, and environmental conservation.

Born in Latvia, became a professional adult in Scotland, and currently raising three wonderfully wild children in northern Portugal while constantly getting lost in weird rabbit holes of information about our complex life in this corner of the universe.

Here's what I'm up to now.
